### ✅ How RCCMS Eliminates Calculation Errors

RCCMS has automatic financial calculations built into every step of the rental process. The system knows your vehicle tank capacities, fuel prices, rental rates, and fee structures. It calculates everything in milliseconds with 100% accuracy, every single time.

#### 1. Automatic Fuel Charge Calculation
*The most common source of revenue loss - fully automated*

**Formula:** `Fuel Charge = Tank Capacity (L) × (Start Fuel % - End Fuel %) / 100 × Price Per Liter`

**Example:** 60L tank, started at 100%, returned at 25%, petrol at AED 4.50/L

**Calculation:** `60 × (100-25) / 100 × 4.50 = 60 × 0.75 × 4.50 = AED 202.50`

**Features:**
- System stores tank capacity for each vehicle in master data
- Fuel prices configurable per fuel type (petrol, diesel, electric)
- Fuel level recorded at pickup and return
- Calculation happens automatically on vehicle return
- Manual override available for exceptional cases (with audit trail)
- No rounding errors - precise to the fils

#### 2. Rental Rate Calculations
*Daily, weekly, and monthly rates applied correctly*

- Automatic rate selection based on rental duration
- Daily rate: Per day pricing
- Weekly rate: Applied when 7+ days (usually discounted)
- Monthly rate: Applied when 30+ days (best value)
- Pro-rata calculations for partial periods
- Seasonal rate adjustments (if configured)
- Vehicle-specific pricing from master data
- Instant calculation as user selects dates

#### 3. Addon Fees & Extra Charges

#### 4. Extra Mileage Charges
*Kilometers matter - track them precisely*

- Odometer reading at pickup automatically recorded

- Allowed kilometers calculated based on rental duration
- Daily limit (e.g., 250 km/day)
- Monthly limit (e.g., 5000 km/month)
- Excess kilometers = Return odometer - Start odometer - Allowed KM
- Charge per excess kilometer configured in settings
- **Example:** Drove 2,500 km in 7 days, limit is 1,750 km (250/day)
- **Excess:** 750 km × AED 0.50 = AED 375 extra charge

#### 5. Late Return Penalties
*Time is money - charge appropriately for delays*

#### 6. VAT Calculations

- VAT-inclusive and VAT-exclusive totals shown

- Compliant with UAE tax regulations

#### 7. Discount & Promotion Handling
