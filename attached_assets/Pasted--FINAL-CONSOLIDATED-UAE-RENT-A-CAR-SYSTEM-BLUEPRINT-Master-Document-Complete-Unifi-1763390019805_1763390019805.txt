# 🟧 **SECTION 0 — SYSTEM PRINCIPLES & SCOPE**

This system covers **every operational, legal, financial, and compliance requirement** needed for a UAE rent-a-car & driver outsourcing business:

- UAE Laws (RTA, Traffic, Tolling)
  
- UAE Toll Systems (Salik, Darb, Aber)
  
- UAE VAT (Tax compliance)
  
- ISO 9001 (Process) + ISO 27001 (Security)
  
- Audit compliance
  
- Insurance & claim processing
  
- Multi-company structure
  
- Branch-centric fleet allocation
  
- Customer, sponsor, corporate, driver workflows
  
- Asset management, depreciation
  
- Accident, fines, risk management
  
- Driver outsourcing
  
- Fleet management
  
- Real operational automation features
  

The blueprint includes:  
**all master data + all operations + all advanced modules + all corrections**.
