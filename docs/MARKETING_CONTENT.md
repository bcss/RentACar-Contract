## One-Liner
A bilingual (English/Arabic) rental car management platform for multi-branch operations, driver services, and complete contract lifecycle management.

---

## Product Overview
KarāraOS is a production-ready rental car management platform built for UAE and GCC markets. It manages the entire rental lifecycle from reservation to return, with built-in support for multi-branch operations, professional driver services, insurance claims, and comprehensive reporting.

---

## Key Features

### Contract Management
- Full rental lifecycle (Reserve → Active → Returned → Closed)
- Automated financial calculations
- Multi-branch support with inter-branch transfers
- Real-time contract status tracking

### Fleet Operations
- Vehicle inspection management
- Maintenance scheduling
- Toll and traffic fine tracking
- Status synchronization across branches

### Driver Services
- Professional driver assignment
- Emirate-aware pricing with surcharges
- Driver scheduling and utilization tracking
- Rate plan management

### Customer Management
- Customer profiles with risk scoring
- Blacklist and watchlist management
- Document expiry monitoring
- Multi-party support (Individual, Sponsored, Corporate)

### Financial Tools
- Deposit management with smart allocation
- Payment tracking (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer)
- Automated invoicing and receipts
- Revenue and expense reporting

### Insurance & Claims
- Incident tracking and management
- Insurance claim workflow
- Damage assessment integration
- Settlement tracking

### Reporting & Analytics
- Financial reports (Revenue, Aging, Cash Flow)
- Operational reports (Utilization, Fleet Status)
- Customer reports (Risk, Retention)
- Exportable to CSV and PDF

### Notifications
- Automated reminders (Expiry, Overdue, Returns)
- Multi-channel delivery (Email, SMS)
- Bilingual templates (English/Arabic)
