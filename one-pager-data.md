## 📸 Inspection System Deep Dive

### Why 95% of Disputes Disappear

**The Problem:**
- Customer: "That scratch was there when I got it!"
- Staff: "No it wasn't!"
- Result: 2-hour argument, waived charge, lost revenue

**The RCCMS Solution:**
1. **Pre-Delivery:** Mandatory 6 photos before activation
   - Front, Back, Left, Right, Top, Dashboard
   - Odometer, fuel level, condition notes
   - Inspector name and timestamp
   - **System blocks activation without photos**

2. **Post-Return:** Mandatory 6 photos before completion
   - Same angles for comparison
   - Return odometer, fuel level
   - New damage notes
   - **System blocks completion without photos**

3. **Visual Comparison:**
   - Side-by-side before/after photos
   - Zoom to see damage details
   - Timestamped evidence
   - **Dispute resolved in 30 seconds**

**RATIONALE:**
- 6 angles = complete 360° coverage (no "you didn't photograph that side" excuses)
- Duplicate detection = ensures unique photos (no uploading same photo 6 times)
- Auto-compression = 10MB → 500KB (efficient storage without quality loss)
- Sequential gates = cannot skip inspections (system enforces compliance)
- Auto-chaining = post-return inspection → fuel charges (eliminates re-entry)

**Real Example:**
Customer disputes AED 1,200 scratch:
- Pull up inspection history (30 seconds)
- Show before photo: no scratch
- Show after photo: scratch visible
- Customer: "Oh right, I backed into a pole"
- Charge collected, dispute resolved

**Time:** 30 seconds vs. 2 hours  
**Revenue:** AED 1,200 recovered vs. waived
